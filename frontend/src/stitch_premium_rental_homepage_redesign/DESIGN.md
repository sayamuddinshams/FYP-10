## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an elevated architectural rental identity for high-altitude living and hospitality across Gilgit-Baltistan. It diverges from clinical, generic real estate portals to convey timeless mountain luxury, bespoke hospitality, and local heritage through a refined contemporary lens.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Architectural & Alpine:** Grounded in dramatic geography—granite ridges, glacial rivers, and artisanal stone-and-timber engineering.
- **Editorial & Discerning:** Curated like an architectural quarterly, balancing high-end photography with poetic typography and quiet whitespace.
- **Trustworthy & Local:** Reliable and reverent of northern communities, conveying verified safety, transparent leases, and regional fluency.

### Design Aesthetic: Alpine Modern Minimalist
The aesthetic pairs deep midnight slate foundations with crisp snow-white cards, frosted glass accents, and warm golden amber highlights. Structural elements use subtle translucent borders and deep ambient shadows that evoke alpine mist and crisp evening light.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the alpine terrain of northern Pakistan: deep midnight slate sky, sunlit warm amber stone, and verdant mountain juniper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0B1320`):** Alpine Deep Slate. Used for core structural dark containers, high-emphasis headlines, hero backdrops, and navigation shells.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / `#E5A93C`):** Karakoram Amber Gold. Deployed selectively for featured badges, premium property highlights, ratings, and active conversion touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Mountain Emerald Sage. Reserved for verification stamps, availability status indicators, positive transaction tags, and eco-amenity chips.
- **Neutral Surface Ecosystem:** 
  - Canvas base: `#F8FAFC` (Glacial Mist)
  - Card and elevated container fill: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Snow)
  - Dark mode and inverted sections: `#14213D` (Sub-slate) and `#0B1320`
  - Subtle borders: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` for light containers; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` for dark containers

### Usage Rules
Avoid heavy solid blocks of secondary amber; reserve it strictly for accents, active states, and prestige visual anchors. Emerald sage must only indicate verified status, active availability, or success messages.

## Typography

This system marries the editorial grace of `Newsreader` (a transitional serif with warm proportions) with the crisp geometry and legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Embellishment
- **Display & Headings:** Use `Newsreader` for prominent hero titles, section headlines, and destination showcases. Pair upright serifs with subtle italic accents on evocative words (e.g., *"feels like home"*, *"curated sanctuaries"*).
- **Body & Structural Text:** Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all listings, amenities, specifications, metadata, and form controls for optimal functional clarity at compact scales.
- **Numbers & Rates:** Render pricing in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` or styled serif currency markers for an upscale architectural magazine rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, rhythmic 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous breathing room reflecting open alpine landscapes.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), compact cards, full-bleed filter triggers, and stacked single-column listing feeds.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, 2rem canvas margins, 2-column listing grids, horizontally scrollable destination carousels.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width 1360px centered, 3-column listing grids, floating multi-parameter search bar with frosted glass styling.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Generous outer section margins maintain high architectural prestige.
- Content groupings inside property cards use tight vertical rhythm (`space-sm` between title and location, `space-md` between specs and pricing footer) to keep data clusters distinct and scannable.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages high-altitude ambient diffusion, frosted glass surfaces, and subtle tonal boundaries rather than aggressive drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Surface Base):** Glacial Mist (`#F8FAFC`) with no shadow.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Property Cards):** `#FFFFFF` fill with an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(11, 19, 32, 0.05)`, bounded by a `1px` translucent border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Hover & Active Overlays):** Lifted property cards and dropdowns transition to `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(11, 19, 32, 0.12)`, with subtle border illumination `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Hero Search Glass):** Frosted glass panels using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, translucent white background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` in light context, or `rgba(11, 19, 32, 0.85)` in dark sections, framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a modern rounded geometric envelope (`roundedness: 2` with selective 2xl scale extensions) to soften rigid architectural photography while preserving balanced proportions.

### Shape Language Specifications
- **Property Cards:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius for main card containers; `0.75rem` (12px) for nested image viewports to produce concentric framing.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) for status indicators (`Verified`, `Featured`), category pills, and tag filters.
- **Search Modules & Bar Overlays:** `1.25rem` (20px) outer radius to convey a bespoke, integrated furniture feel.
- **Form Inputs & Action Buttons:** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) matching tactile button states.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book/Search/Explore):** Alpine deep slate (`#0B1320`) background, crisp white label, `0.75rem` border radius, subtle hover elevation shift, and optional micro-arrow transition.
- **Prestige Accent Action:** Warm amber gold (`#D97706`), text in `#FFFFFF`, used for high-intent conversions ("Schedule Viewing", "List Your Property").
- **Secondary Ghost:** Outlined in `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`, dark text, background shifting to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` on hover.

### 2. Status & Metric Badges (Pills)
- **Verified Status:** Full pill, emerald green background tint (`#ECFDF5`), deep sage green text (`#047857`), paired with an emerald check icon.
- **Featured / Prestige:** Warm amber tint (`#FEF3C7`), rich gold text (`#B45309`), paired with a mini star or diamond glyph.
- **Property Type Tag:** Frosted slate tint (`rgba(11, 19, 32, 0.7)` on dark, `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` on light) with crisp micro-typography.

### 3. Property Cards
- 16:10 or 4:3 high-resolution architectural image container with corner radius `0.75rem`.
- Floating badge cluster top-left; interactive wishlist heart button top-right in frosted circle (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, white `rgba(255,255,255,0.85)`).
- Price block styled with high-emphasis typography, followed by `/month` or `/night` in muted slate.
- Spec icons (beds, baths, square footage) displayed in horizontal row separated by muted dot dividers.

### 4. Search Filter System
- Unified floating search bar with segmented compartments: Location picker, Property Type, Budget Range, and a prominent Slate Search trigger.
- Quick district pills below the main bar ("Hunza", "Skardu", "Gilgit", "Nagar") with active state highlighting.

### 5. Input Fields & Select Controls
- Crisp snow fill (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, transition to `1px solid #0B1320` or `#D97706` on focus with subtle outer glow `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.
- Clean label positioning and integrated icon adornments.
